Capabilities

Built for real operations

Every feature was designed for the daily reality of fuel distributors and transport companies.

NF-e XML Import
Auto-fill the entry form by uploading an XML invoice. Driver, plate, base, fuel type, quantity, and unit price extracted automatically.
Full Analytics
7 analytical tabs: monthly, per-driver, per-vehicle, per-fuel, comparative, distribution, and price evolution — all with Chart.js.
Freight Calculation
Configure R$/L rates per company. Automatic freight totals by plate, driver, company, and vehicle set — with historical composition support.
Rich PDF Exports
Custom logo per company, accent colors, margins, fonts, per-month page breaks, and a footer. Managed managerial monthly report included.
Multi-user Permissions
Roles: Supremo, Admin, Operator. Per-company access. Login via e-mail or @username. Change history on every entry, per user.
